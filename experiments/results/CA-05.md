One measured cold/warm pair, not a percentile or performance threshold. The
population is exactly 100 000 words of deterministic LLM-built synthetic text,
not corpus; fixed vocabulary, 8–16 words per sentence and 5–12 sentences per

a section ID. No expert judgment or quality reference is involved.

Cold means an empty temporary LMDB store and empty segmentation cache, not
flushed operating-system disk caches. Warm means the same document regenerated
in the same process with retained segmentation and vector caches. Every one of
9374 warm chunks was fetched with VectorStore.get; a warm miss raises. Timers
include synthetic generation, segmentation/chunking, content hashing, cache
lookups, cold stub embedding and synchronous per-entry puts. Store open/close
and JSON writing are outside the timers. store_bytes is live encoded key/value
bytes, excluding LMDB pages, indexes and bookkeeping.

HashEmbedder is a stdlib stub, not a model; the number measures segmentation +
chunking + cache overhead only, not model inference (EM-02/SV-02). It includes
stub hashing/normalization cost. No private corpus, model, GPU, quality gate or
keystroke-to-signal latency (IR-04) was exercised. Host load and temporary-store
filesystem affect timings; the full test suite ran concurrently with this run.

chunk_blocks using SegmentationCache/segment_sentences, then SHA-256 VectorKeys
feed VectorStore.get and bounded same-level embed/put batches. Unwired from the
